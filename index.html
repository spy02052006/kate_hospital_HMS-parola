FAQ

Common questions

How can I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form on this page and choose "Send request on WhatsApp". The hospital will confirm your appointment with you. You can also call 90753 60600.

How can I contact the hospital?

Call or WhatsApp 90753 60600. For emergencies, call the same number immediately.

What are the OPD timings?

OPD timings are to be confirmed by the hospital. Please call 90753 60600 before you visit.

How can I find the hospital?

We are at V4C8+59C, Kajgaon Rd, Balaji Nagar, Parola, Maharashtra 425111. Use the Get directions button in the contact section.

Which doctors are available?

Dr. Atul Kate (M.D., General Medicine), Dr. Mahesh Kate (M.S., Gynaecology & Obstetrics) and Dr. Sneha Kate (B.A.M.S., DGO). Their availability is confirmed when you book.

How can I enquire about diagnostic tests?

Call or WhatsApp us with the test you need. We will confirm availability, sample requirements and report timings.

What maternity services are available?

Pregnancy and maternity care, delivery and postnatal care, and women's health services. Antenatal guidance and monitoring are provided for mother and baby.
