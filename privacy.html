Privacy notice

How we handle your information

What we collect

When you request an appointment on this website, we collect the details you enter: your full name, mobile number, chosen department and doctor, preferred date and time, and any short non-medical enquiry you choose to add.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to respond to your appointment request and to contact you about it by phone or WhatsApp. We collect only what is needed for that purpose.

Medical information

Please do not send medical reports or detailed health information through this website or WhatsApp. Share these with the doctor during your consultation or visit.

How the request is sent

The website does not store your request on a server. When you choose to send your request, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message to the hospital. You can review and edit that message before sending. Sending it is your decision.

Keeping and deleting information

Retention periods and deletion requests are to be defined by the hospital. To ask for a copy or deletion of your information, contact us on 90753 60600.

Your consent

You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting the hospital. Withdrawing consent does not affect care you have already received.

Website analytics

This website does not include analytics or tracking tools in this version. If the hospital adds any, this notice will be updated and sensitive form fields will not be recorded.

Last updated: to be confirmed by the hospital. Applicable Indian data protection requirements apply.
